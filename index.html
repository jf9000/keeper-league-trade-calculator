Keeper League Trade Calculator for Fantasy Football

Not redraft. Not dynasty. Keeper.

A free trade calculator built for fantasy football keeper leagues. Connect your Sleeper league to load rosters, draft picks and keeper costs, or enter them manually, then see which side of a trade comes out ahead.

How it works

Player values come from average draft position (ADP) and current-season production. Keeper costs and draft picks are factored in, so a cheap keeper is worth more than the same player at full price.

What you can do

  • Grade any trade for both teams, and share it as a link or an image.
  • See which players to keep on any roster, given each keeper's draft-round cost and the picks you own.
  • Find trades that help both teams, and rank every team in your league.
  • Watch players, and see who is rising and falling in value each week.

Frequently asked questions

Is the keeper league trade calculator free?

Yes. No account or API key is needed.

Do I need a Sleeper account?

No. Connecting a Sleeper league loads your settings, rosters, traded picks and keeper costs automatically, but you can also enter everything by hand.

How is a keeper valued differently from a normal player?

A keeper is worth the player's value minus what it costs you to keep them, such as the draft round you give up. A player with a low keeper cost is worth more in a trade than the same player at a high cost.

What is a keeper league?

A keeper league sits between redraft and dynasty: each season you keep a limited number of players from your roster, usually at the cost of a draft pick, and redraft the rest.

How do I value draft picks in a keeper league trade?

Early-round picks are worth more because they cost more to replace with a keeper. This calculator values each pick by round and year, and counts the picks a team still owns.

Where does the player data come from?

ADP from MyFantasyLeague and Fantasy Football Calculator, plus player stats and league data from Sleeper. ADP is refreshed weekly.
